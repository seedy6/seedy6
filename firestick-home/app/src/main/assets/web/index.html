Make Welcome Home your home screen

Fire TV doesn't list this switch in its own menus, so it's switched on once over Wi-Fi with ADB. An Android phone on the same Wi-Fi can do it.

  1. On the Fire Stick: Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → ADB debugging → On.
  2. On your phone, install Bugjaeger (or another ADB app) from the Play Store and connect to , port 5555.
  3. When the TV asks Allow USB debugging?, tick Always allow and choose OK.
  4. In the app's shell, run these two lines: settings put secure enabled_accessibility_services com.seedy6.welcome/com.seedy6.welcome.HomeRedirectService settings put secure accessibility_enabled 1
  5. Turn ADB debugging off again afterwards.

Press Back to close. This tile disappears once the switch is on.
